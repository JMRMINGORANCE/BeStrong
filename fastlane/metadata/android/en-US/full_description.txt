BeStrong is a strength training app for Android and Wear OS, completely free: no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no paid features and no ads. Everything you see is available to everyone from day one.

And it gets better every day: your suggestions reach us straight from the app and, with the help of AI, good ideas become new features in no time. Many of the new features in each version were requested by users themselves.

Log your sets in seconds, follow a program that progresses on its own and see how you improve. No accounts, and your data stays only on your device.

100% FREE, FOR REAL
• No purchases, no subscriptions, no “Pro” version and no ads.
• Every feature, program and stat, for everyone.

AN APP THAT GROWS WITH YOU
• Send your ideas from Settings (the light bulb icon) and follow their status without leaving the app.
• Thanks to AI, suggestions are reviewed and brought to life in days, not months.
• New versions very often, with what the community asks for.

TRAIN
• Set table with weight × reps, reps only or time, plus the “Previous” column showing what you did last time.
• Warm-up, normal, drop and failure sets, RPE, notes and tempo for each exercise.
• Automatic rest timer when you complete a set, also in the notification with the screen off.
• Automatic warm-up and plate calculator (kg or lb).
• Supersets and circuits, focus mode and “next exercise” with a countdown.

PROGRAMS AND ROUTINES
• Ready-to-go programs: Full Body, Push / Pull / Legs, Upper / Lower, 5×5 Strength, Glutes and legs, Body recomposition and more, or a plan based on your goal.
• Programs with automatic progression: 5/3/1 Boring But Big and GZCLP.
• Progressive overload: suggests adding weight when you complete every set, and a deload when you stall.
• “Today” on the home screen, with the loads already worked out.
• Create, duplicate and reorder your routines, or save a workout as a routine.

YOUR PROGRESS
• Personal records, celebrated when you finish your workout.
• Charts for each exercise: estimated 1RM, top weight, volume and reps.
• Weekly muscle map with effective sets per muscle and balance between movement patterns.
• History with calendar, compare two workouts and a journal with tags.
• Body measurements with a weight chart.
• Share your workout as an image.

WEAR OS WATCHES
• Works with any Wear OS 3 or later watch: Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch and many more.
• Train from your watch or your phone: the workout is shared live and you can switch between them.
• Live heart rate and calories, rest with vibration and tempo guidance.
• Complete a set with the physical button and adjust the weight with the bezel or crown.
• Tile and watch face complication.

FOLDABLES
• Designed for Galaxy Z Fold: list and detail side by side, side panel during workouts and tabletop mode.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• Works offline and without signing up.
• Backups to the folder you choose, plus CSV export and import.
• Optional Health Connect: your workouts show up in Samsung Health.

Plus: exercise library with images and technique tips, custom exercises, widget, reminders, light or dark theme and your choice of accent colour.

Got an idea? Send it from Settings: it could be in the next version.
